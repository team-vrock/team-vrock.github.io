This privacy policy explains how https://www.vrock.ch handles data when you visit the site.
<!--more-->

### Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics only after you approve analytics cookies in the cookie notice. Analytics helps us understand which posts are useful and how the site can be improved.

When analytics is approved, Google may process technical information such as browser type, approximate location, referring page, visited pages, and interaction data. You can read more in Google's privacy information at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Cookies

The site stores a consent cookie after you approve analytics. This cookie records your choice so the notice does not appear on every page view.

### Site Search

The site uses Algolia for post search. Search requests are sent to Algolia when you use the search box. Do not enter personal or confidential information into the search box.

### Contact

If you contact TEAM VROCK through LinkedIn or another external platform, that platform's own privacy policy applies.

### Your Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. For privacy questions, contact us through the public TEAM VROCK channels linked on this site.

### Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, analytics setup, or search functionality changes.
